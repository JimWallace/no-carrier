### Added

- **EXFILTRATION.** A stealth contract: a TARGET node holds a 6 to 12 MB
  file. Breaching it starts a TRANSFER that moves at the machine's baud
  rate while the ICE grows stronger each turn. LOGOUT with the file and
  JACK OUT; the payout tier (clean, full, half) follows the Alert state.
- **Four new cards** on EXFILTRATION loot: COMPRESS, SCRUB.SH, GHOST.DMN
  and NULL ROUTE.
- **The sim plays EXFILTRATION** and reports payout tiers.
