### Added

- **`carrier play`.** One full contract: a generated network of 9 or 10
  nodes on a 40-column map, one action per tick, node ownership, RELAY,
  REPAIR, VIRUS and DATA nodes, PAYLOAD carrying for sysop KERNELs, sysop
  intents (CONTEST, HARDEN, SPAWN ICE, SWEEP) and REINFORCE, loot after
  each capture, DATA hardware upgrades, and THE OPERATOR on ROOT. Win by
  capturing ROOT; lose to Trace, Integrity 0 or a lost ENTRY.
- **Contract replays.** `carrier play --record FILE`; `carrier replay`
  reads both kinds of replay.
