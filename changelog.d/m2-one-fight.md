### Added

- **`carrier fight`.** One playable node fight in a 40-column green
  terminal: draw, cycles, exploits with weakness tags, proxies, scripts,
  daemons and RAM, security layers with carry-over, PAYLOAD against the
  KERNEL, four ICE programs with telegraphed intents, Trace, PROBE and
  LOGOUT. Cards play by number key or by typed name with Tab completion.
- **Replays.** `carrier fight --record FILE` and `carrier replay FILE`
  reproduce a session exactly.
- **Rule tests and golden transcripts** for every fight rule.
