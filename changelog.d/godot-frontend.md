### Added

- **A Godot frontend, a first sketch.** `godot/` holds a SwiftGodot
  extension and a Godot 4.7 project with a map screen (click a node to
  attack it) and a battle screen (your rig, the ICE and their next moves,
  the machine's layers, your hand as cards). It runs the same engine as
  `carrier play`. CI builds it for macOS 13 and later as the
  `no-carrier-godot-macos` download; see `godot/README.md`.
